# Scientific limitations and interpretation

## Proxy limitations

- Root-zone soil moisture is one drought-related dimension, not a universal drought classification.
- Burned area does not alone identify ignition cause, fire severity, ecological impact or wildfire status.
- NDVI measures vegetation greenness and can be affected by seasonality, snow, cloud, land management, canopy structure, disturbance and sensor quality.

## Spatial limitations

A 0.5° cell may contain several land-cover types, elevation regimes and ecological boundaries. Fractional-area regional weighting reduces boundary misallocation but does not remove within-cell heterogeneity or the modifiable areal unit problem. RESOLVE 2017 boundaries are static.

## Temporal limitations

Monthly observations can conceal short-lived events and ordering within the month. Forecast labels are exact monthly outcomes; they do not resolve daily event sequence. The 1981–2000 soil-moisture reference and 2001–2016 NDVI/fire training reference involve different periods and assumptions.

## Statistical limitations

Adjacent cell-month observations are spatially and temporally dependent. A spatial-tile × year block bootstrap is an approximation; tile sizes and alternative dependence structures require sensitivity analysis. Rare events can leave some regions unscorable. Calibration and discrimination can vary by biome, source coverage and event prevalence.

## Use limitation

The project is retrospective and exploratory. It is not a validated operational warning system or a causal model. Outputs must not be treated as ecological diagnosis, emergency guidance or conservation decisions without independent domain review and stronger evidence.
